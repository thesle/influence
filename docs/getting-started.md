# Getting Started

This guide walks through the first steps after Influence is installed and
running.

## Logging in

Open the Influence web application in your browser and sign in with the
credentials your administrator provisioned. A session cookie keeps you signed in
until you log out or the session expires.

## The content hierarchy

Content in Influence is organized in three levels:

1. **Spheres** — top-level containers that also define who can read or write the
   material inside them. Circle a Sphere to pin it to the top of your list.
2. **Facets** — a tree of topics within a Sphere. Facets can be nested and
   reorganized by moving one under another.
3. **Polygons** — individual markdown documents that live inside a Sphere and,
   optionally, a Facet.

## Authoring a Polygon

Create a Polygon inside a Sphere, then edit its content as markdown. While you
edit, an edit lock prevents two people from overwriting each other; the lock is
released when you finish or after a period of inactivity.

You can:

- Link to other Polygons so references stay current when a target is renamed.
- Paste images directly into the content.
- Encrypt a selected span so its text becomes an obfuscation token, readable only
  by users with reveal permission for the Sphere.

## Finding content

- **Search** looks across every Sphere you can access.
- **Polygons I've Created** and **Recently Viewed** give quick, personal lists.
- **Bookmarks** let you save Polygons for later, grouped by their Sphere.

## Exporting

A Polygon can be exported to PDF for sharing outside the platform. Encrypted
spans stay masked in the export unless you have reveal permission.
